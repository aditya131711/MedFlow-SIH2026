 🏥 MedFlow
Unified Digital Patient Journey Platform
Capture information once. Validate clinically. Carry the context throughout the patient journey
MedFlow is a unified digital patient journey platform designed to connect key stages of a patient's hospital journey — from registration and triage to doctor consultation, e-prescription, pharmacy verification, and patient records.
Built as an **SIH 2026 MVP**, 
MedFlow focuses on reducing fragmented workflows, repeated information entry, and loss of patient context across different hospital roles.

🚑 Problem
Hospital workflows often involve multiple disconnected stages and roles.
Patient information may be repeatedly entered or transferred between registration, triage, nursing, consultation, prescription, and pharmacy workflows.
This can result in:
- Repeated data entry
- Fragmented patient information
- Loss of clinical context
- Delays in patient movement
- Disconnected role-based workflows
- Limited visibility across the patient journey

💡 Solution
MedFlow connects the major stages of a patient's journey into one unified workflow.

Patient Registration
        ↓
Symptoms & Vitals
        ↓
Protocol-Based Triage
        ↓
Nurse Validation
        ↓
Queue Management
        ↓
Doctor Consultation
        ↓
E-Prescription
        ↓
Pharmacy Verification
        ↓
Patient Record
